Tournaments and the leaderboard

The game runs a repeating tournament lasting three days. You earn points by playing — raiding, attacking and building — and points scale with the bet you played at. Reaching a milestone unlocks a reward you claim from the tournament board.

Claim a reward before the tournament ends. When a tournament ends, the score and every unclaimed reward are cleared and the next one starts from zero. Tournament rewards are in-game items only and are covered by the section above: they are not money and have no value outside the game.

The leaderboard shows the name and face you chose alongside your island number and star count. If you would rather not appear, play as a guest.

If you do sign in, keep access to the Apple or Google account you used, because that is what proves the island is yours. Connecting a second sign-in in Options is what lets you move between an iPhone and an Android phone.

Four things protect this, and they are all in the game rather than in a promise:

  • Messages are filtered before they are posted. A message containing a word on the block list is refused outright — it is not starred out and it does not reach anybody.
  • Every message somebody else wrote carries a report flag. Tapping it sends the message and its author to me. I review reports and act on them within 24 hours, which can mean removing the message, withdrawing that player’s access to chat, or closing their account without notice or refund.
  • Reporting also blocks, immediately and in both directions. You stop seeing anything that player writes, they stop seeing anything you write, and neither of you is offered the other as a raid target again. It takes effect at once and does not wait for me to review anything.
  • Nothing is broadcast beyond your clan. There is no public chat, no direct messages, no way to reach a stranger, and no way to attach a photo, a file or a link.

Messages are kept for three days and then deleted automatically. Reports are kept for longer so that a pattern of behaviour can be seen; see the Privacy Policy.
