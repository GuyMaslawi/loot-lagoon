Tournaments and the leaderboard

The game runs a repeating tournament lasting three days. You earn points by playing — raiding, attacking and building — and points scale with the bet you played at. Reaching a milestone unlocks a reward you claim from the tournament board.

Claim a reward before the tournament ends. When a tournament ends, the score and every unclaimed reward are cleared and the next one starts from zero. Tournament rewards are in-game items only and are covered by the section above: they are not money and have no value outside the game.

The leaderboard shows the name and face you chose alongside your island number and star count. If you would rather not appear, play as a guest.

Connecting a second sign-in in Options is what lets you move between an iPhone and an Android phone.
