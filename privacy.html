Loot Lagoon — Privacy Policy

Effective 22 August 2026

SupportPrivacy Policy

The short version. Loot Lagoon collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. Your island is saved on your own device and never leaves it. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking of any kind.

Who this is about

Loot Lagoon is published by Guy Maslawi, an individual developer in Israel. Questions about this policy or about your data go to guymuslave@gmail.com.

What is collected

Nothing. The app does not gather your name, your email, your contacts, your location, your photos, your microphone, your camera, your device identifiers, or any advertising identifier. It contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, and no third-party tracking library.

What is stored, and where

Your game state — coins, spins, island level, buildings, cards, collections, quest progress, notification preferences and a record of which purchases have been delivered to you — is written to a file inside the app’s private storage area on your device. iOS keeps that area sandboxed from other apps.

That data stays on the device. It is not uploaded, backed up to any server of mine, shared, sold, or transmitted to any third party. I have no way to read it.

Tracking

Loot Lagoon does not track you. It does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA), does not request App Tracking Transparency permission, and does not build a profile of you or link any information to your identity. The app’s privacy manifest declares NSPrivacyTracking = false.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never receive, see or store your payment card, your billing address, or your Apple ID. What the app records is only which purchases Apple has confirmed, so that a pack interrupted mid-delivery can be handed over on the next launch. That record is a file on your device.

Apple’s handling of a purchase is governed by Apple’s Privacy Policy, not this one.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, they are scheduled and delivered locally by your own device. There is no push server. Nothing about you is sent anywhere in order to produce them, and you can turn them off at any time in the game’s Options or in iOS Settings.

Signing in

The current iOS release has no sign-in and no accounts. You play as a guest and your island belongs to the device. If a future version adds sign-in, this policy will be updated before it ships.

Network use

Ordinary play makes no network requests. The app talks to the network only when you make a purchase, and then only to Apple.

Children

Loot Lagoon contains simulated gambling and randomised paid items. It is rated 17+, is not directed to children, and is not part of the Kids category. I do not knowingly collect information from children — and since the app collects no information from anyone, there is none to collect.

Your rights

Because everything lives on your device, you are already in full control of it. Options → Delete Account erases your save, your profile and every backup copy immediately; deleting the app does the same. There is no server-side copy for me to delete, export or correct on your behalf.

If you are in the EEA or the UK, this means there is no personal data processing for me to be a controller of. Write to me anyway if you have a question — I will answer it.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting or transmitting anything, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed before that version is released.
